Grasshopper
An interactive introduction to gradient ascent

You are a grasshopper on a hill you cannot see.

Somewhere along this landscape there is a highest point, and the view from up there is worth the climb. The trouble is your eyesight. You can see the ground for a few centimetres around your feet and nothing further. No summit, no horizon, no map. All you get is the little patch of slope you are standing on.

From that patch you choose two things and only two things: which way to jump, and how far. Then you land somewhere new, look down again, and choose again. Get to the top in as few jumps as you can.
